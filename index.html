About

My path started outdoors. I studied forestry engineering at the Technical University of Madrid, with an Erasmus year at the Czech University of Life Sciences in Prague, and spent summers on a helicopter-borne wildfire crew and mapping tree inventories. A master's in geodesy and cartography then took me into surveying: drone photogrammetry, digital terrain models and field work with robotic total station and GPS.

At Spain's National Geographic Information Center (IGN-CNIG) I moved from the field to the web. I worked on the Spanish Spatial Data Infrastructure (IDEE), researching vector tile services, building WMS and WMTS services, implementing INSPIRE services and presenting the work at international conferences. Along the way I also studied computer engineering at UNED.

Since 2020 I've been at CARTO, the Agentic GIS platform. I grew from support engineer to senior support engineer and technical account manager, building GIS and data science proofs of concept, installing and maintaining self-hosted deployments and running trainings. Since July 2024 I've led the support team, making sure customers get clear answers to hard geospatial questions. I also program with AI agents such as Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini to automate user use cases with CLIs and to automate internal support processes.
